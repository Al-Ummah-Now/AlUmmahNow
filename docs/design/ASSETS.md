# Theme assets

Every theme asset is in `packages/fanous-tokens/themes/<id>/assets/`. No stock or third-party artwork is used.

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Logo, wordmark, favicon and app icons | Al Ummah Now's own `logo.png` and wordmark, resized or tinted, some on a theme-coloured plate | Project-owned |
| Open Graph images | Composed from the logo and wordmark | Project-owned |
| Sign-in graphics, patterns, loaders | Hand-built SVG drawn from the lantern's shapes | Project-owned |
